PRACTICAL ADVICE FOR FOREIGNERS

Travel Essentials & Getting Around

Everything you need to know about train routes, buses, taxi tips, and emergency helplines.

How to Get to Andong

  • From Seoul (KTX-Eum): Takes ~2 hours from Cheongnyangni Station (청량리역) directly to Andong Station (안동역).
  • From East Seoul Bus Terminal: Express buses run every 30 mins (approx 2.5 hours).
  • From Busan: Mugunghwa Train or Intercity Bus (~2.5 to 3 hours).

Getting Around Town

  • Bus #210 to Hahoe Village: Departs from Andong Station / Bus Terminal. Runs every 30-40 minutes.
  • T-Money Card: T-Money and major foreign credit cards are widely accepted on city buses.
  • Taxis & Kakao T: Taxis are affordable in Andong. Use the Kakao T app or show our Korean Taxi Cards!

Tourist Assistance & Helpline

  • 1330 Korea Travel Hotline: Free 24/7 translation & tourist support in English, Chinese, Japanese. Dial 1330.
  • Emergency Services: Police (112), Fire & Ambulance (119).
  • Tourist Info Center: Located right outside Andong Station Exit 1. Free English maps & brochures available.
